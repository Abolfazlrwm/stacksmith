SERVICE LEVEL OBJECTIVES
- Three SLOs: Availability 99.5% over 30 days, Latency P99 under 500 ms over 5 min, 5xx under 2% over 5 min. - Each SLO is wired to an alert in Alertmanager — availability and 5xx are fast-burn critical, latency is warning. - The 30-day error budget at 99.5% is roughly 3.6 hours of allowed downtime per month. - Both fast-burn (2-min) and slow-burn (30-day exhausted) alerts route with severity. - A deploy that returns 5xx for 30% of requests but keeps the health endpoint green trips SLOHigh5xxRate — it would slip past an outage-only alert.
