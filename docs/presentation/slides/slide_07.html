PROVEN IN CI
- 27 CI workflows run on every push — these are the most important drills, not the full list. - deploy.yml does blue→green→blue with canary probes; canary-failure deliberately breaks green to prove auto-rollback. - restore-drill, encryption-drill, and disaster-recovery-drill form the backup/DR chain — verified end-to-end. - The DR drill destroys the stack on a CI runner and rebuilds from an encrypted backup; if it passes, DR is a tested procedure, not a slide. - Latency, SLO metrics, chaos, and k6 load tests keep the operability promises honest on every push.
