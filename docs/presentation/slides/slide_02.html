THE PROBLEM
Teams want a chat assistant over private documents. They get two bad options.
cloud_off 1.  A SaaS chat product

Convenient, but you must upload every document to a third party. A non-starter for legal, medical, financial, or any sensitive corpus.

data leaves your machine
build 2.  Self-host an open-source model

No data leaves, but now you are an SRE. TLS, backups, deploys, monitoring, alerting, disaster recovery — before the product is usable. Most teams underestimate this by an order of magnitude.

you become a part-time ops team
There is no middle ground today.
Either your data leaves, or you become an operations team.

  • The two options are bad for opposite reasons: one leaks data, the other forces you into operations.
